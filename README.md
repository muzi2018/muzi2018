### Research & Engineering Interests

- **Whole-Body Motion Control (System 0)**: Reinforcement learning locomotion, whole-body control (WBC), MPC, and fall recovery across **humanoid, quadruped, and wheeled-legged** robots.
- **Embodied Manipulation & Loco-Manipulation (System 1 & 2)**: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, bimanual dexterous manipulation, and VLM/world-model-grounded task planning.
- **Human-to-Robot Transfer & Physical Foundation Models**: Learning generalizable motor priors from human demonstrations for adaptive real-world interaction.

### Featured Robotics Systems & Demos

> Full demo videos are available on my **[Homepage → Projects](https://muzi2018.github.io/)**.

- **General-Purpose Humanoid Robot Platform** — Whole-body balancing, compliant control, and dynamic bimanual manipulation on a full-size humanoid ODM platform.
- **Vision-Based Quadruped Loco-Manipulation** — Coupling onboard perception with whole-body control for multi-terrain locomotion and manipulation.
- **Wheeled-Legged Last-Mile Delivery Robot** — Hybrid wheeled efficiency and legged obstacle negotiation across curbs, steps, and indoor/outdoor transitions.
- **VLA-Based Long-Horizon Workpiece Sorting** — End-to-end closed-loop bimanual sorting over extended task horizons.
